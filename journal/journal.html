What is it?

A real journal that travels from friend to friend. Each person adds creative pages, then passes it along.

🎨

What do you add?

Anything! Art, collage, drawings, writing, photos, stickers, pressed flowers, doodles…

👖

Inspired by…

The Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants — but make it art journals!

✏️ How it works
1
🫂 A group gathers — 3 close friends or 10+, whatever works
2
✂️ Each person gets a journal & fills their pages however they want — no rules!
3
📬 Mail it (or hand it off!) to the next person in the group
4
🏠 The journal comes home filled with everyone's magic ✨
